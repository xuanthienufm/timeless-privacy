Privacy policy · Timeless

Publisher: Tesseract Lab · Thien Bui
Privacy and support: tesseractlab.studio@gmail.com
Updated: 12 November 2026

Timeless processes photographs on your device. There is no Timeless account, advertising or server that receives your photos. Google Play Billing uses internet and network-state access to check prices, purchase Premium and restore purchases. Editing, library management and image optimization do not require uploading photographs.

The free version includes five successful exports. Premium is available as a one-time Lifetime purchase or an automatically renewing monthly subscription through Google Play. Eligible monthly subscribers can receive a three-day free trial. The purchase screen shows the current offer, the price after any trial or introductory period, and renewal terms before you confirm. You can cancel renewal through Google Play; access continues while Google Play reports your subscription as active.

When you import a photo, logo, .cube LUT or .zip LUT collection, Timeless reads the files you select in Android's file picker and stores a copy in its private storage. Photos, imported LUTs, existing image metadata, editing settings, versions, templates, thumbnails and frequently used looks are stored locally. If you select a file from a cloud-storage provider, that provider's own policy applies.

Premium payments are handled by Google Play and your Google account. Timeless does not receive or store card numbers, bank details or your Google password. The app sends product and purchase requests to Google Play. Google and the billing SDK process purchase information and technical data under Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Timeless does not include photographs, EXIF, filenames or library contents in billing requests.

Signed purchase receipts, the most recent subscription verification time and the export counter are stored privately on the device to check Premium access. Lifetime purchases support offline use. Monthly access is cached for up to 24 hours after verification and then requires another check with Google Play. Restoring purchases requires a connection to Google Play with the account that made the purchase. Removing an exported image from the list does not reset the counter. This version does not use a Timeless server to verify purchases or synchronize the free-export counter across devices.

Your language preference is stored on your device. On Android 13 and later, the app uses Android's per-app language setting. On older versions, it stores the choice in private app preferences. Changing language does not upload photographs or change your original files, editing settings or purchase ownership.

On installations from Google Play, Timeless can check for app updates while the library is visible. The Google Play In-App Updates SDK and Play Store handle availability, background downloads and installation. Google documents processing device metadata, the application version, and installed module/asset-pack information for this purpose: https://developer.android.com/guide/playcore/in-app-updates . No photographs, EXIF, editing settings or photo-library filenames are included in Timeless update requests. Reminder deferrals are stored only on the device. Timeless offers a flexible update and requests installation/restart only after you choose it from the library. Google Play may separately update apps according to your Play Store settings.

When you tap Download LUTs, Timeless retrieves public ZIP files directly from Sony's CDN (77snszqv.media.zestyio.com). No photographs, photo-library filenames or image metadata are sent. Sony and its CDN receive technical request information, such as the IP address and requested LUT URL, under their own policies. Timeless verifies each file and stores imported LUTs privately for offline use. No downloads start automatically. Source links open your browser, which has its own policy.

Existing image metadata may include capture time, camera model, GPS coordinates or serial numbers. GPS and serial fields are not selected for photo frames by default. When you choose to show these fields, they become visible in the exported image. Timeless does not request device-location permission to read metadata already present in a file.

Exports are saved to shared device storage. When you choose Share or Open with, Android passes the file to the app you select. Further handling is governed by that app's policy.

You can remove imported photos, edited versions and exported items from the library. Removing an item from Exports does not delete the external file unless you separately select the option to delete it from the device. Removing an imported copy does not change the source file outside Timeless.

Clearing app data or uninstalling Timeless removes its private library, imported LUTs, versions, templates, local receipts, counter, language choice and other preferences. Source files and exported images outside the app remain. The Exports list may not be restored after reinstallation. Google Play continues to manage your Premium purchase, which can be restored using the account that made it. Automatic Android app-data backup and transfer are disabled in this version.

When you choose Rate Timeless, a native bottom sheet offers Rate on Google Play, Later, and Don’t remind me. Google Play processes the rating and optional review under its own policies. Timeless cannot read whether you submitted a review or its stars; closed-test feedback may be private to the developer. First-use time, successful editor-export count, last offered version, cooldown and opt-out are stored only on your device. Automatic invitations require sufficient usage, a later app version and a cooldown. Photographs, EXIF, filenames and editing settings are not sent in a rating request.

Privacy policy · Timeless

Publisher: Tesseract Lab · Thien Bui
Privacy and support: tesseractlab.studio@gmail.com
Updated: 24 October 2026

Timeless processes photographs on your device. There is no Timeless account, advertising or server that receives your photos. Google Play Billing uses internet and network-state access to check prices, purchase Premium and restore purchases. Editing, library management and image optimization do not require uploading photographs.

Closed beta 0.21.2 disables purchases and the five-export limit. It does not initialize Google Play Billing or send billing requests. The purchase and receipt handling described here applies to commercial builds when Premium is enabled.

When you import a photo, logo, .cube LUT or .zip LUT collection, Timeless reads the files you select in Android's file picker and stores a copy in its private storage. Photos, imported LUTs, existing image metadata, editing settings, versions, templates, thumbnails and frequently used looks are stored locally. If you select a file from a cloud-storage provider, that provider's own policy applies.

Premium payments are handled by Google Play and your Google account. Timeless does not receive or store card numbers, bank details or your Google password. The app sends product and purchase requests to Google Play. Google and the billing SDK process purchase information and technical data under Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . Timeless does not include photographs, EXIF, filenames or library contents in billing requests.

Signed purchase receipts and the export counter are stored privately on the device to check Premium access and support offline use. Restoring purchases requires a connection to Google Play with the account that made the purchase. Removing an exported image from the list does not reset the counter. This version does not use a Timeless server to verify purchases or synchronize the trial counter across devices.

Your language preference is stored on your device. On Android 13 and later, the app uses Android's per-app language setting. On older versions, it stores the choice in private app preferences. Changing language does not upload photographs or change your original files, editing settings or purchase ownership.

The Sony LUT link opens Sony's website in your chosen browser. That browser and website have their own policies. Timeless does not download LUTs automatically or send photographs to Sony.

Existing image metadata may include capture time, camera model, GPS coordinates or serial numbers. GPS and serial fields are not selected for photo frames by default. When you choose to show these fields, they become visible in the exported image. Timeless does not request device-location permission to read metadata already present in a file.

Exports are saved to shared device storage. When you choose Share or Open with, Android passes the file to the app you select. Further handling is governed by that app's policy.

You can remove imported photos, edited versions and exported items from the library. Removing an item from Exports does not delete the external file unless you separately select the option to delete it from the device. Removing an imported copy does not change the source file outside Timeless.

Clearing app data or uninstalling Timeless removes its private library, imported LUTs, versions, templates, local receipts, counter, language choice and other preferences. Source files and exported images outside the app remain. The Exports list may not be restored after reinstallation. Google Play continues to manage your Premium purchase, which can be restored using the account that made it. Automatic Android app-data backup and transfer are disabled in this version.
